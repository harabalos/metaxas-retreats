PILLAR 4 — GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
           Rank in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

AI systems answer questions by pulling from the most cited, clearest sources.
To appear when someone asks ChatGPT "best glamping in Greece":

  1. ENTITY CLARITY
     Keep NAP identical everywhere online:
       Name:    Metaxas Retreats
       Address: Mikros Gialos, Poros, Lefkada, 31082, Greece
       Phone:   +30 6973219980
       Email:   metaxasretreats@gmail.com

  2. BE CITED IN ARTICLES
     If 10+ travel blogs mention you by name, AI systems learn you exist.
     This is why the backlink strategy above directly feeds GEO too.

  3. EXPAND FAQ CONTENT ON-PAGE
     Current FAQ is only in schema (invisible to users).
     Add a visible FAQ section to the homepage and blog articles.
     AI systems love direct question-answer format.

  4. YOUTUBE VIDEO
     A 2-min "Tour of Metaxas Retreats" uploaded to YouTube with keyword-rich
     title and description gets you into Google's video index.
     Google AI Overviews regularly feature YouTube videos.
     Title example: "Glamping in Lefkada Greece — Metaxas Retreats (Sea View)"

  5. WIKIPEDIA-STYLE CLARITY
     Structured headings (H1, H2, H3), clear direct answers, no fluff.
     AI systems scrape clean, structured content.
